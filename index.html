05 · FAQ

Questions, answered

What's a natal chart?

A natal chart is a snapshot of exactly where the planets were positioned at the moment you were born. It holds powerful information about the energies you were exposed to at that exact moment, and reading it reveals a lot about your personality, patterns, and the themes that tend to show up in your life.

What's included in a reading?

Every Natal Chart and Solar Return reading includes a personalized in-depth report and a live Zoom session where I walk you through your chart in detail — the recording is yours to keep. Tarot readings cover two questions, answered live during a Zoom/video call.

How long does turnaround take?

Turnaround depends on my booking load that week. Once you've booked, I'll confirm your specific timeline directly so you know exactly when to expect your reading.

What happens after I pay my deposit?

Once your 50% deposit is in, you'll fill out a short birth-info form (date, time, and place of birth). I'll then prepare your reading and get a Zoom call booked with you to walk through it together.

Do you offer refunds?

The deposit is non-refundable, and once I've begun working on your reading, the full amount is non-refundable — this covers the time and preparation every reading takes. You do get a free follow-up consultation for up to 6 months after your reading if anything needs clarifying.

Is this a substitute for professional advice?

No. Everything I offer is for self-reflection and personal insight — it isn't a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial advice, and any decisions you make based on a reading are your own.
